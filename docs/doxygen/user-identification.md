
# User identification 

## Registration

\image html registrationScreen.png "Registration page screenshot"

On the registration page, fill in all required fields:

- Username
- Email address
- Company name
- Manager name
- Password

After clicking **Sign In**, you will receive an email containing the activation link for your account.

## Log in
Once your account is activated, go to the login page and sign in with your email address and password.

After submitting your credentials, you will receive a 2FA code by email. Enter the 6-digit code to complete the login process.
